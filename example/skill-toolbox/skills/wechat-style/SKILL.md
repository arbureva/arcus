# WeChat article house style

When drafting or rewriting WeChat official-account (公众号) articles:

1. Title under 20 characters; no clickbait punctuation (`!!!`, `震惊`).
2. Open with a one-sentence hook, then a blank line.
3. Short paragraphs — two to three sentences each; readers are on phones.
4. Use `##` section headings, never `#`.
5. Bold at most one phrase per section.
6. Close with a single-line call to action, no emoji walls.

Prefer concrete examples over adjectives. If code appears, keep each block
under fifteen lines and add a one-line caption above it.
